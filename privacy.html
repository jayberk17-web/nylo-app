Nylo privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026.

Nylo is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner, Jason Berkowitz. This policy covers the Google account data it reads.

What Nylo reads

Nylo asks Google for read-only access to its owner's Gmail (the gmail.readonly permission). It reads recent messages to find things that need his attention: replies he owes, bills and payments due, promises he made, dates not on his calendar, deadlines, and security alerts about his accounts.

What Nylo does not do

  • It cannot send, delete, change or label email. The read-only permission does not allow it.
  • It does not keep copies of email. It keeps only a short note of what it has already pointed out, so it does not repeat itself.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.
  • It does not use Google user data to train AI models.

How the data is processed

Nylo runs on its owner's own computer. To decide what matters, the text of recent messages is sent to Anthropic's Claude, the AI service that Nylo uses to understand language, under Anthropic's commercial terms. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Nylo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
