Nylo

Nylo is a personal voice assistant that Jason Berkowitz built for his own use. It runs on his own computer and serves one person only. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

With his permission, Nylo reads his Gmail (read-only) to remind him of things that need his attention, such as bills coming due, replies he owes and dates that are not yet on his calendar.
